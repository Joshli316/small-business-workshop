✓ 好处✓ The Upsides

  • 自己做主Be your own boss
    自己安排时间，不用看老板脸色Set your own schedule, no boss
  • 收入上不封顶No income ceiling
    努力可以转化为更多收入Your effort directly grows your income
  • 不需要英语流利English not required

  • 家人可以一起做Family can join

⚠️ 真实的代价⚠️ Real Tradeoffs

  • 收入起伏不定Income goes up and down
    头一两年常常没工资 — 全靠存款First 1–2 years often no paycheck — live on savings
  • 没有福利No employee benefits
    医疗保险、退休、带薪假期 — 全部自己掏钱Health insurance, retirement, vacation — all out of pocket
  • 早起晚归Long hours
    每周 60–80 小时很常见 — 周末也得开60–80 hour weeks common — weekends too
  • 失败率高Many businesses fail
    约一半生意撑不过 5 年 — 别拿房子抵押~50% close within 5 years — don't mortgage your home
💡 小建议：先在原工作上「兼职试水」 6–12 个月，再决定要不要全职做。💡 Tip: Test your business as a side hustle for 6–12 months before going full-time.

✓ 建议先做 DBA（个人独资 + 店铺名）✓ Best first step: DBA (sole prop with a business name)

⚠️ Warning: California LLCs owe an $800/year minimum tax whether you profit or not — heavy for new businesses.

什么是 DBA？What is a DBA?
"Doing Business As" — 在您本人名字之外起一个店铺名字。例如：王大明做家庭清洁，想叫「Sunny Cleaning」，就要登记 DBA。在 LA 县申请，4 周内要登报纸。 "Doing Business As" — using a business name different from your legal name. Example: Wang Daming runs cleaning as "Sunny Cleaning" — that needs a DBA. File at LA County, publish in newspaper within 4 weeks.
什么是 EIN？What is an EIN?
"Employer ID Number" — 联邦税号。开公司银行账户必须有 EIN。免费在 IRS 网站申请，15 分钟拿到（中文页面：irs.gov 输入「中文」）。 "Employer ID Number" — your federal tax ID. Required to open a business bank account. Apply free on irs.gov — 15 min, instant approval (中文 available).
⚠️ 没有 SSN 也能申请⚠️ No SSN? Still OK
用 ITIN（个人税号）也可以申请 EIN 和 DBA。IRS 表 SS-4 上选「未提供 SSN」即可。Seller's Permit 同样接受 ITIN。 You can apply with an ITIN — use IRS Form SS-4 and check the no-SSN option. CA Seller's Permit also accepts ITIN.
省钱建议Save Money
不要找代办公司花 $300+ 注册 — 全部步骤可以自己做，总费用不到 $80。CSC 可以免费帮您填表。 Don't pay agencies $300+ to register — all steps can be done yourself for under $80. CSC will help you fill the forms for free.

报税Taxes

自雇税 — 您要自己存税 Self-Employment Tax — Save It Yourself

老板不扣税 — 您要每季度自己交给 IRS No employer to withhold taxes — you pay IRS quarterly
自雇税 15.3%SE Tax = 15.3%
社保 12.4% + 联邦医保 2.9% = 15.3%。打工时老板帮您交一半，但自雇要全部自己交。 Social Security 12.4% + Medicare 2.9% = 15.3%. As an employee, employer pays half. Self-employed = you pay both halves.
用 Schedule C 报税File with Schedule C
报税表 1040 + 附件 Schedule C（生意收支）+ Schedule SE（自雇税）。所有开销都在 Schedule C 上抵扣。 Form 1040 + Schedule C (business income/expenses) + Schedule SE (SE tax). All expenses deduct on Schedule C.
季度预交Quarterly Estimated Tax
如年利润超过 $400，每季度交一次：4/15 · 6/15 · 9/15 · 次年 1/15。不交会罚款。 If profit over $400/year, pay quarterly: Apr 15 · Jun 15 · Sep 15 · Jan 15. Penalties if you skip.
✓ 可抵扣的开销✓ Deductible Expenses
店租、库存、车油、手机、广告、保险、外包人工。家庭办公室：屋里专门工作的房间也能抵扣（按面积比例）。 Rent, inventory, gas, phone, ads, insurance, contractors. Home office: a dedicated work room can be deducted (% of home).
